Bot.i

Move, turn, jump, repeat. Bot.i is a coding puzzle game for kids. Snap blocks together to guide a little robot to the flag, one step at a time. Each chapter adds a new idea: loops, functions, IF / ELSE, recursion, sensors, variables, lists, events and two bots running at once. Every program can be seen as real Python or JavaScript.

  • 24 chapters, a daily puzzle and Endless levels made from what you've learned
  • A level editor: build your own puzzles, test them, fix them and share them with a code
  • Skins, victory dances and secrets to find, all earned by playing
  • Free to start. One purchase unlocks the full game, behind a grown-ups check
  • No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Nothing about your child leaves the device
